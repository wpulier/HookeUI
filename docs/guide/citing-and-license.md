# Citation, license and scope

## Cite the version you used

When reporting work performed with HookeUI, identify the software and the exact
version or commit. Link to its release record and describe the actual methods.
A practical software-reference format is:

> HookeUI contributors. *HookeUI* (version used) [Computer software].
> https://github.com/wpulier/HookeUI. Accessed: your access date.

Replace the placeholders with your experiment's version and date. For a source
checkout, include the full commit SHA. Use the
[Stable release record](https://github.com/wpulier/hookeui.org/releases) or
[Beta release record](https://github.com/wpulier/hookeui-beta/releases) for the
installer you used. Do not cite a moving `latest` link as if it identified fixed
experimental software. Repository citation metadata is in
[CITATION.cff](https://github.com/wpulier/HookeUI/blob/dev/CITATION.cff).

Also cite the underlying models, algorithms and datasets according to their
authors' instructions. HookeUI does not replace those citations. Retain method
versions, settings, calibration and any manual corrections alongside your results.
No journal paper or DOI is asserted by this software-reference format.

## Research scope

HookeUI is a scientific imaging and research tool. These guides and example
results do not establish clinical validation. Do not use outputs as a substitute
for clinical, diagnostic or therapeutic judgment. Validate each method for your
own data and intended research use, and distinguish exploratory output from
independently verified findings.
