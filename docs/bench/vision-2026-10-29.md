# Vision: Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon beside the Gemini defaults (2026-10-29)

The numbers behind the `claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-opus-5-5-fast` and `gemini-4-argon` vision options. Each run had the
current defaults beside it in the same hour, because provider latency drifts by the hour. Claude ran at effort `low` (Opus 5.5
always thinks; low is its fastest); fast mode is Anthropic's `speed: "fast"` beta at twice the price. The research it
answers, and the architecture it feeds, are in hooke-web `docs/research/2026-10-29-live-partner/`.

## Grounding: "what's that?" on the real leaf recordings

`python -m bench.grounding --strategies box --repeats 3`: the 15 hand-labelled questions × 3, sequential.

| Option | Right object | p50 | p95 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Opus 5.5 (fast mode) | 44/45 | 0.94 s | 1.58 s |
| Gemini 3.5 Flash (minimal) | 45/45 | 1.46 s | 2.01 s |
| Claude Opus 5.5 | 43/45 | 2.29 s | 3.91 s |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash (low) | 45/45 | 4.19 s | 6.70 s |
| Gemini 4 Argon (low) | 45/45 | 4.57 s | 10.9 s |

Every Claude miss was the same question, "What's the dark speck on the left edge?". It named it right ("dust or debris")
and boxed it beside the labelled point. An earlier run of 15 × 2 the same day gave Opus fast mode 59/60 at 0.93–1.03 s,
Opus 30/30 at 2.4 s, Argon 30/30 (p95 19 s), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (thinking off) 27/30, GPT-6.1 Terra (`none`) 22/30 and
Claude Haiku 5.5 (thinking off) 14/30. The last three are not options.

## Naming microscopy (µBench)

`python -m bench.naming.run`: the whole set, 180 pictures (leucocytes, Pap cytology) in the eyepiece disc × 4 conditions
(told the right specimen, nothing, the wrong specimen, and a blank grey disc told the specimen), through both paths: a
click (`identify`) and the running look (`sight`). The per-condition table is in `naming.md`.

The Anthropic account ran out of credit near the end (from row 6,702 of 7,200 every Claude call answered "Your credit
balance is too low"); after the top-up those 192 Claude rows were rerun with the same seeds, and all answered. The table
compares every option on the 171 pictures every option answered (9 lost a Gemini row to an error): exact names over the
three told conditions (348 answers; only the leucocyte set has exact names), the right kind (513), ECE when told the
right specimen, p50, and blank discs named over every grey answer.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini 4 Argon / click | **80%** | 79% | 0.22 | 5.2 s | 0/180 |
| Gemini 4 Argon / look | **69%** | 84% | 0.18 | 9.2 s | 0/180 |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash / click | 63% | 80% | 0.31 | 3.5 s | 0/180 |

Paired (McNemar) on the same answers:
- **Argon is the most exact:** ahead of Opus (75 to 10 on a click, p < 0.001; 55 to 30 looking, p = 0.009) and of 3.8
  Flash (66 to 7, p < 0.001). On the right kind, Argon does not differ from either.
- **Opus against 3.8 Flash:** better looking (40 to 13, p < 0.001); level on a click (27 to 33, p = 0.52).
- **Opus fast mode against 3.5 Flash:** more exact looking (45 to 24, p = 0.015), more often the right kind on a click
  (36 to 20, p = 0.044), far better calibrated, and it never named a blank disc.
- **Fast mode against standard Opus:** no significant difference (11 to 19 looking, p = 0.20).

This corrects the 60-picture sample earlier the same day, which put Opus ahead of 3.8 Flash on a click (70% against 64%).
Before the rerun, with Claude rows missing, Opus looked worse than 3.8 Flash on a click; with every row it is level.
Claude had no refusals in 2,880 microscopy calls: all answered after the rerun except 2 unreadable replies.
The adapter now says Anthropic's own reason for a failed call instead of a bare HTTP status.

## What it means

No default changes in this PR. The candidates are:
- **Quick look on a click:** Opus 5.5 in fast mode: grounding 44/45 at 0.94 s (3.5 Flash: 45/45 at 1.46 s), naming at
  least as well as 3.5 Flash, far better calibrated, never naming an empty eyepiece. Against that: twice standard Opus's price.
- **`look_hard`:** Argon is the most exact by a wide margin, at 5–9 s p50 and an 11 s grounding p95, so the voice must say
  the wait. Opus 5.5 is the 5 s option: the best calibrated, and clearly ahead of 3.8 Flash on the look path.
- **Small things:** Gemini 3.8 Flash and Argon find platelets better than Claude.

A default moves after a hard-look set (counts, compare pairs, mitosis crops) is measured with decision rules written
before the run.
