# Vision: Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon beside the Gemini defaults (2026-10-29)

The numbers behind the `claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-opus-5-5-fast` and `gemini-4-argon` vision options. Each run had the
current defaults beside it in the same hour, because provider latency drifts by the hour. Claude ran at effort `low` (Opus 5.5
always thinks; low is its fastest); fast mode is Anthropic's `speed: "fast"` beta at twice the price. The research it
answers, and the architecture it feeds, are in hooke-web `docs/research/2026-10-29-live-partner/`.

## Grounding: "what's that?" on the real leaf recordings

`python -m bench.grounding --strategies box --repeats 3`: the 15 hand-labelled questions × 3, sequential.

| Option | Right object | p50 | p95 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Opus 5.5 (fast mode) | 44/45 | 0.94 s | 1.58 s |
| Gemini 3.5 Flash (minimal) | 45/45 | 1.46 s | 2.01 s |
| Claude Opus 5.5 | 43/45 | 2.29 s | 3.91 s |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash (low) | 45/45 | 4.19 s | 6.70 s |
| Gemini 4 Argon (low) | 45/45 | 4.57 s | 10.9 s |

Every Claude miss was the same question, "What's the dark speck on the left edge?". It named it right ("dust or debris")
and boxed it beside the labelled point. An earlier run of 15 × 2 the same day gave Opus fast mode 59/60 at 0.93–1.03 s,
Opus 30/30 at 2.4 s, Argon 30/30 (p95 19 s), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (thinking off) 27/30, GPT-6.1 Terra (`none`) 22/30 and
Claude Haiku 5.5 (thinking off) 14/30. The last three are not options.

At 60 pictures and one repeat, differences under about 10 points are within noise. Claude had no refusals and no errors in
240 microscopy calls; the adapter sets server-side fallbacks and says a refusal as a refusal.

## What it means

No default changes in this PR. The candidates are:
- **Quick look on a click:** Opus 5.5 in fast mode (the fastest accurate, the best calibrated). It weighs against Gemini
  3.5 Flash's price and its precision on tiny things.
- **`look_hard`:** Opus 5.5, with Argon as the "most exact, take your time" option.

A default moves after the full naming run and a hard-look set (counts, compare pairs, mitosis crops) are measured with
decision rules written before the run.
