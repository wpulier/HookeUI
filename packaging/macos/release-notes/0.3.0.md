# HookeUI Beta 0.3.0 — the workspace, made clear

This Beta is the whole-app usability pass: one Tab model, research that reads at a glance and at scale, Models and
Library as one fast sheet, Data as the one place a Project's work goes, and a first launch that explains itself. The
scientific workflow of 0.2.3 (import → segment → correct → measure → table → analysis → graph → animate → export →
save → reopen) is unchanged underneath; this release changes what you see and how you get there.

## What changed

- **Tabs.** Every Tab in the row has one shape and one selected look. A researcher's or Study's Tab is a Tab like
  the others; its × closes the Tab and never stops the researcher. The row's one control is **+**: New Tab, New note,
  New researcher, New study…, All Tabs…. A closed Tab comes back from All Tabs… or ⌘⇧T.
- **Research.** **New researcher** opens a Tab with one box; Start sends your words to the Assistant (the one way a
  researcher starts), and the Tab becomes the researcher the moment the app lists it, never before. Stop reads
  **Stop requested** until the app confirms. A keep-going question offers **Keep going** / **Not now**. From five
  researchers the Researchers drawer is a board (Needs you · Working · In line · Done, Stop selected); four or more
  researcher Tabs fold into **Researchers (n)**. A queued researcher's ring is still, not spinning.
- **Models and Library** open as one centred sheet over the dimmed Stage, with no blur: categories with counts on the
  left, search and × in the header, where-it-runs chips, visual cards with one action each, and a model's Details as a
  pane beside the grid. Escape closes the inner layer first. One phrase: **Not on this Mac**. A setup sheet opened from
  Tools returns to the Project when closed. **Spending** is in the Hooke menu.
- **Data** is where a Project's work goes: its **Inputs** first, then its **Results**, newest first. A card opens on
  click; **Add to Tab…** is its secondary action, the same words as everywhere else; **How it was made** says who made
  it, with the ids under Technical details. Results arrive as the app makes them; what you have not seen is marked
  **New** and counted on the Data badge. A researcher's result opens its Tab; its lessons and presentations are cards
  of their own.
- **First launch.** The empty Stage says what Hooke is for and offers a picture or video, the live camera or the
  guided example. The Assistant's key note appears only once something needs the key, in plain words. A keyless
  failure says what to do. Help speaks the shell's words.
- **Library** uses the word list (inputs, recordings and pictures, Projects) with three clear actions: Open in this
  Project, Open in new Project, Move to trash.

## Verified on this candidate

- Studio typecheck, the unit suite (3,045 tests) and the integrated `make check` gate on the frozen source.
- Feel, measured headless at 1440×900: Models sheet 26 ms (p95 41), Library 26, Data 23, a researcher Tab 29 ms
  from click to paint; no layout shift in the Tab row during use.
- Live, with real keys on an isolated instance: a picture dropped in, the Assistant answered on it, a researcher
  started from the New researcher Tab, took over its Tab, finished, and its result appeared in Data marked New;
  a Study draft was created and honestly reported its providers as not set up; InstanSeg (16 MB) was set up from
  Models with its progress in Activity.
- The packaged DMG: first install in a clean macOS VM and the 0.2.3 → 0.3.0 update rehearsal against a private feed
  (receipts under `~/MicroscopeMind/release-preparation/`).

## Scope and limitations

- Stage zoom, Present mode for lessons, and the Studies card's redesign wait for the next Beta.
- The Study's providers (Claude managed research, Gemini tool use), GCP staging and cloud recovery are not qualified
  in this release; the Study says so and Start stays off until they are set up.
- A researcher's finished-at and its lessons as Data entries are derived in the app until the server records them;
  the server's own words for a keyless Assistant and a finished researcher are still being changed.
- Owner acceptance of the installed Beta is recorded separately in ticket 62.
