# HookeUI Beta 0.5.0 — Data you can read

This Beta reorganises Data and research results around one rule: clicking anything in Data opens it on the right of
the Tab you are on, in one reader that looks the same for every kind. Nothing moves you to another Tab unless you ask
(an open Study or New researcher draft gives way to the work Tab under it).

## What changed

- **Data, by what each thing is.** Pictures and recordings, Analyses, Write-ups and Sources, as a lab notebook keeps
  them, then Other folded last. Who made each thing is a filter, Made by everyone, not a section. A run's outlines,
  table and answer are one row ("Bright regions, 271 regions"). A row on the Tab you are on says On this Tab. A
  write-up says its current form, such as Presentation or Findings v2.
- **One reader.** A row opens on the right of your Tab, in the Assistant's place: its title, who made it and when, and
  two views, the thing itself and How it was made. Outlines add Show on picture, which draws them on the Stage. A
  researcher's work adds Talk to Researcher N, the one action that goes to its Tab. Open in new Tab gives anything a Tab
  of its own. Every way a row opened before is still there: its window on this Tab in the reader's ⋯, and a
  researcher's Tab through Talk to Researcher N. Below 1200 px the reader takes Data's place,
  with ‹ Data to go back.
- **A researcher's work is in Data.** Its report (with its presentation as one of its forms), its tables and charts,
  lessons, videos, generated pictures and the sources it cited are rows of their own, each naming it. Sources are their
  own kind, each with where it came from and, once its report cites it, the write-up that cites it.
- **How it was made** says only what was recorded: who made it and when, what it was made from, the model or method
  and its settings, the versions and the sources. A record that does not say who made it shows no maker.
- **Researchers.** A researcher that has made something says what ("Made a report and a table"), leading to its work
  in Data.
- **A researcher's Tab is for talking to it.** Its conversation on the left, and on the right the same reader, whose
  title switches between everything it made and the sources it cited. The column of files on the left is gone: what it
  made is in that switcher, and what it read is in How it was made. A step's link opens in this reader, without leaving
  the Tab.
- **Older results Tabs.** A "Researcher N results" Tab made by an earlier version now says that researcher's results
  open in its own Tab, with Open Researcher N and Close this Tab. Nothing in it is lost.
- **The Assistant opens what Data lists the same way**, in the reader on the Tab you are on, and says it is open only once the
  reader shows it. Outlines it shows are still drawn on the picture. A researcher's notice now says its work is in Data,
  where before it could say its work "was sent to the viewer" when nothing was sent.
- **Add to Tab… stays where you are** and says "Added to Tab 2", with Go to Tab 2.

## Verified on this candidate

- The release gate on this source: the Python suite in CI, the studio's type check and its 3,506 tests, and the
  simulated-camera checks of the live view, research and actions. The live view under load was run beside 0.4.1's
  source on the same heavily loaded Mac: 15 to 16 fps and a delay of 149 to 151 ms, against 0.4.1's 15 to 16 fps and
  153 to 154 ms, both at the 150 ms limit there.
- Every sentence of these notes checked against the code by a release audit, and its corrections made; one was in the
  code: the reader no longer says You for an older run the Assistant made.
- The reader, Data by what each thing is, the researcher's Tab and the older-Tab pointer, each built beside its frame
  of the approved design and shot on real research sessions.
- The packaged DMG: the update from 0.4.1 to 0.5.0 in a macOS VM, through Restart Now.

## Scope and limitations

- **Who made older results is not known.** Runs from before this version do not record whether you or the Assistant
  started them, so they show no maker, and Made by offers You and the Assistant only once a record names them.
- In the reader, Move to trash… for a picture or recording you added takes you to the Library, where Move to trash
  asks first; for everything else it is shown, unavailable, until a later version.
- After an update, models that passed Hooke's own check on an earlier version show **Not checked** until their check
  runs again on this build.
- Every other limitation listed for 0.4.1, apart from how 0.4.1 itself was installed, still applies.
