# HookeUI 0.1.0, a preview for friends

- Open the bundled example (a real microscope image of human cells), find its bright objects and see their measurements. No account, key or camera needed.
- Open your own pictures and videos.
- HookeUI keeps itself up to date: new versions download in the background and install when you quit.

# Known issue
- After Try an example, if the stage still says "What will you look at?", click the picture under Assets on the left; the walkthrough then carries on.
