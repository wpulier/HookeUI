# HookeUI Beta 0.4.0 — the workspace, alive

This Beta redesigns how the workspace looks and feels: a calm instrument where the specimen is the brightest thing on
screen and anything working draws the eye by itself. The scientific workflow underneath is unchanged, and every control
0.3.0 had is still reachable. Closed Tabs are under ⌄, and Activity is in the side bar and in Data.

## What changed

- **The lit cell.** One sign for the work of researchers, runs and setup, after Hooke's own cork cell: gold while
  something works, green when it is ready, blue with a dot when it needs you. It replaces the spinning rings,
  coloured words and dots in Tabs, the Working strip, the chat, Researchers, Tools and Models.
- **Tabs.** The Tab row holds only Tabs, and Main is always first. Researchers you start open as Tabs beside yours,
  never grouped into one pill. One that is working, waiting or needs you stays in the row; when the row runs out of
  room, finished ones go under ⌄ first. Closing a Tab shows where its work went, to Researchers or Data, and nothing
  stops. ⌄ lists every Tab, closed ones too, with the layout's status and recovery copies.
- **The Stage bar.** Under the Tabs, on every Tab: what is on the Stage, then the camera, Record and the Assistant.
- **The Working strip.** A fixed row under the Stage with a pill for each researcher and run that is working, needs
  you or finished unseen, plus the performance meter and Spending. The Stage never resizes as pills come and go, and
  × on a pill only hides it.
- **The left side.** One drawer with one header. A tool's controls are a page of Tools, with ‹ Tools to go back, and
  the page waits where you left it while you look elsewhere. Researchers is one list in sections (Needs you, Working,
  Done) with answers in the row. Data shows Inputs, then Results, as rows.
- **Running a model.** Every model, method and Fiji function you run from Tools gets the same run page, built from its
  catalog folder: what it runs on, the model, what it makes, only the settings it declares, where results go, and one
  primary action: Run with the model's name, or Click in the view for a task you do by clicking. A model that is not set up turns that
  action into its one step, such as a download, a key or Fiji, and it becomes Run when ready. Running shows elapsed
  time and the run's own step words, never a made-up percent. Use it here in Models opens the model's run page.
- **Results on the picture.** A run on the picture Main shows now draws its outlines on that picture, with the layer's
  controls beside it, and keeps the result in Data.
- **The chat.** A card for each researcher appears the moment the app lists it, lit in the chat, on its Tab and in the
  strip at once. The answer arrives in place. The chat follows new lines only while you are at the bottom; "New
  messages" waits when you have scrolled up. One composer holds the specimen, + to attach, the model, Talk and Send.
- **The researcher Tab.** What the researcher made and its outline on the left, its conversation in the middle with
  one composer, and on the right the work itself as a page, with one status line saying what is happening to it now.
  A Study is one home in the same shape.
- **Models.** Cards in one shape with one action each, and counts that agree with Tools. Add model or tool starts from
  three plain choices and ends on the new entry's run page.
- **Plain words when a researcher cannot run.** A researcher whose provider key is missing is refused at once, naming
  the key and where to add it. A researcher on an OpenAI account with no credits stops at once and says so.

## Verified on this candidate

- The integrated release gate: the Python suite, the studio's type check and its 3,381 tests, the native checks and
  the 38 simulated-camera scenarios. Two timing checks that missed under heavy load on the Mac passed when run alone.
- Every sentence of these notes checked against the code by a release audit, and its fixes reviewed independently.
- Live, with real keys on an isolated instance (no camera; a picture on Main): a researcher asked for in the chat
  showed its card about 3 s later, with its Tab and its pill, and reached Findings ready in 39 s; its Tab showed what
  it made, its conversation and its page. A run on Main's picture drew 271 outlines on it with no extra window, and
  Check it here opened the model's check.
- The packaged DMG: first install in a clean macOS VM, and the 0.3.0 → 0.4.0 update rehearsal against a private feed.

## Scope and limitations

- After an update, models that passed Hooke's own check in 0.3.0 show **Not checked** until their check runs again on
  this build. Press Check it here on the model and run the check; trying a model on your picture does not clear Not
  checked. On Click to outline, a model other than the current one needs its check before you can click with it.
  Keeping a passed check across builds is proposed for a later Beta.
- Outlines drawn on the picture are not drawn there again after the app restarts; the result stays in Data, where
  Show masks opens it with its outlines.
- One outline layer is drawn on the picture at a time. The three newest earlier layers stay listed with Show, and Show
  swaps them; every layer stays in Data.
- On a Gemini account that has run out of credit, a researcher shows its model call failing and retries twice before
  it stops; only OpenAI's no-credit answer is recognised at once.
- Under heavy load on the Mac, switching Tabs, opening the Tools drawer and opening Models can take a little longer
  than the design's budget (one measurement under load: p95 60, 110 and 139 ms against 50, 100 and 120).
- In the light appearance the gold of a working cell is fainter than the 3:1 contrast a graphic should have; the words
  beside it always say what it is doing.
- Tools' Can be set up rows show each entry's description on one line until catalog entries gain a short task name.
- Stage zoom and Present mode for lessons, listed for this Beta in 0.3.0's notes, wait for a later Beta.
- The Study's providers (Claude managed research, Gemini tool use), GCP staging and cloud recovery are still not
  qualified; the Study says so and Start stays off until they are set up.
