# HookeUI Beta 0.4.1 — clear restarts, honest launches, smooth panels

This Beta fixes three things from 0.4.0: the restart sheet after an update, the launch screen, and how the workspace
moves when a side panel opens or closes. It also offers a few small models once, so a first launch does not download
anything you did not choose.

## What changed

- **One clear restart sheet for an update.** After Install and Relaunch, or Restart to update, Hooke first reads what
  a restart would stop ("Checking your work before restarting…"), then asks one question. With nothing at stake it
  asks "Restart Hooke to finish updating?", with the version, how long a restart usually takes and where your data is
  kept; Restart Now is the default (Return) and Later is Escape. With something running it asks "Restart Hooke and stop
  what's running?", naming what is running in plain words (six at most, then how many more), with Stop and Restart or
  Later and no default. With unsaved work, a lost connection or a read that fails, it does not restart and says why.
- **Later means later.** After Later, the side bar says Restart to update and Settings says the new version is
  downloaded; either asks again. Check for Updates… in the app's menu asks the same way.
- **One rule for every quit.** Once the workspace is open, ⌘Q, closing the window and logging out all check first:
  with nothing at stake Hooke quits; with work running it asks "Quit Hooke and stop what's running?"; with unsaved or
  unreadable work it asks, with Quit Anyway. A waiting update installs as Hooke quits.
- **The launch screen says only what this launch does.** An ordinary launch shows no stages, only "Opening your
  workspace" and what it is starting. An update whose scientific software is unchanged says so and downloads
  nothing. A stage is ticked only after its step ran, and a stage with nothing to do disappears.
- **Asked once before the first download.** A first launch asks before it downloads Hooke's scientific software, with
  the size this build measured: Python and 126 packages, about 310 MB to download and about 1.2 GB on disk, from
  pypi.org and GitHub. Download and Open, or Quit. An update never asks.
- **A few small models, offered once.** On the empty Stage and at the top of Models: "Add a few small models?", with
  EdgeTAM (56 MB) and DINOv2 small (88 MB) ticked, InstanSeg (16 MB, plus its own software) and Cellpose-SAM (1.2 GB,
  non-commercial use only) to choose, Bright objects shown as included, and the folder they are kept in (in Models,
  under Choose). Download uses the same queue and progress as Models; Not now hides the offer, and Models
  has them whenever you want them.
- **Smooth side panels.** Opening or closing Data, Activity, Researchers, Inputs, the Tools list or the Assistant moves
  the panel, the Stage and its picture together in one short motion, instead of the Stage snapping and the picture
  jumping. The picture stays inside the Stage while it moves. With Reduce motion on, panels land at once. The workspace also does
  much less work while idle.

## Verified on this candidate

- The integrated release gate on this source: the Python suite (12,905 passed), the studio's type check and its 3,435
  tests, the native checks of the restart sheets and the launch screen, and the simulated-camera scenarios. Two timing
  checks that missed under load (the frame rate while media work runs, in the full run, and clicks answered while
  following, after the live-under-load scenario) passed when run alone.
- Every sentence of these notes checked against the code by a release audit, and its seven corrections made.
- The panel motion, measured in a browser at 1440 × 900 and 1100 × 800: the Stage moves in 9 to 12 steps within the
  200 ms motion, the picture never passes the Stage's edge, no frame after the press takes over 20 ms, and one press
  does one layout write instead of up to five.
- The packaged DMG in clean macOS VMs: a first install (the question before the download, the launch stages, the
  small-models offer), the 0.4.0 → 0.4.1 update, and an update from this candidate to a later test build that shows
  the new restart sheet, Later and Restart Now.

## Scope and limitations

- **This update is installed by 0.4.0**, so its restart sheet, Later and quitting work as in 0.4.0 one last time. The
  new ones apply from the next update.
- After an update, models that passed Hooke's own check on an earlier version show **Not checked** until their check
  runs again on this build. Press Check it here on the model and run the check.
- Models opens over the workspace, as before, so the Stage does not move for it. A tool's page docked beside the Stage
  slides in when you bring it back from Tools, and lands at once otherwise.
- The panel motion was measured in a browser at 60 Hz; it has not been measured on a 120 Hz display.
- Every other limitation listed for 0.4.0 still applies: outlines drawn on the picture are not drawn there again after
  a restart (they stay in Data); one outline layer is drawn at a time; a researcher on a Gemini account with no credit
  retries twice before it stops; the gold of a working cell is faint in the light appearance; Tools' Can be set up rows
  show each description on one line; Stage zoom and Present mode for lessons wait for a later Beta; and the Study's
  providers are not yet qualified.
