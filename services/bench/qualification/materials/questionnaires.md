# Questionnaires (H14 study)

These are reported but decide nothing (design §7.4).

- **Single Ease Question (SEQ)**, after each attempt: "Overall, how difficult or easy was this task to complete?" on
  1 (very difficult) to 7 (very easy).
- **System Usability Scale (SUS)**, once per app at the end of session 2: the standard 10 items (Brooke, 1996),
  verbatim from the original publication, 5-point agreement scale, scored the standard way (0–100). The source is
  freely usable with acknowledgement. Obtain the exact item text from the original source and keep it, hashed, in
  the binding manifest's materials.
- **NASA Task Load Index, raw (unweighted)**, once per app block: the six subscales (mental demand, physical demand,
  temporal demand, performance, effort, frustration), each on 0–100 in steps of 5. Use NASA's published form; take
  the mean of the six as the raw TLX.

Record answers in the attempt log (SEQ) or the session's questionnaire sheet (SUS and TLX), against the participant
code only.
