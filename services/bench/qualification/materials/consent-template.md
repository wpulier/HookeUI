# Consent form template (for the ethics review; not yet approved)

This is a template for the institutional ethics review or exemption that the design requires before the pilot
(§2.4). The reviewing body's own form and wording take precedence. Nothing here is legal advice.

**Study:** comparing two programs for viewing and analysing microscopy images (napari and Hooke).

**What you will do:** two sessions of about 2 hours 15 minutes each, 1 to 7 days apart, at [site]. You will work on
image tasks in each program, answer short questionnaires, and think aloud if you're comfortable doing so.

**Recording:** your screen and voice are recorded. Recordings are stored under a participant code, not your name, and
are seen only by the study team and authorised reviewers who check the results. [Retention period and storage
location, set by the ethics review.]

**Payment:** [amount, set by Will per design §2.4] per completed session hour, including screening and travel time.
You are paid for the time you take part even if you stop early.

**Voluntary:** you may stop at any time without giving a reason, and ask for your recordings to be deleted until
[date the data are locked].

**Risks:** minimal; ordinary computer work. Breaks are scheduled, and you may take more.

**Contact:** [study lead], [ethics body].

Signature, date, participant code.
