# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and welcoming to beginners. Critique ideas and evidence,
not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Attribute sources, distinguish results from speculation, correct substantive errors,
and share only material you may disclose. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful
content and restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explain
which behavior needs to change.

Use GitHub's private reporting controls for abuse or sensitive security information;
do not reproduce the offending private material in a public issue. Scientific
questions and corrections belong in ordinary discussions or issues.
