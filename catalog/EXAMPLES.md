# Catalog example inputs

The three `example-synthetic-texture.png` inputs are deterministic geometric RGB
textures generated for interface and shape checks. They are not microscope images
and provide no evidence of biological quality or equivalence to earlier private
recordings. New installation proofs bind their actual bytes.

Spot-measure/spot-shape inputs and the Swin2SR default grid are generated geometric
fixtures. The basic-bright-objects human-cell crop derives from the CC0 specimen
documented in `assets/examples/NOTICE.md`. No model weights are included here.
